Clean your oven with 2 pantry items — no harsh chemicals 👇

1. Take out the racks (wash them separately)
2. Mix 1/2 cup baking soda + about 3 Tbsp water into a paste
3. Spread it over the grime — skip the heating element
4. Let it sit at least 12 hours (overnight)
5. Wipe it out with a damp cloth + a plastic scraper
6. Spray vinegar on leftover paste, let it fizz, wipe clean

⚠️ Oven off and cool, wear gloves, don't scrub the door gasket, never mix with oven cleaner or bleach — and if you have a self-cleaning oven, check your manual first.
