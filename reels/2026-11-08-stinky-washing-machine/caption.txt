Washer smells musty? It's not your clothes — it's hiding in the seal 👀

1. Pull back the rubber fold and pick out hair, lint and lost items
2. Scrub the seal with a diluted bleach solution (mixed per the label)
3. Pull out the detergent drawer, soak it in hot soapy water, brush and rinse
4. Run the Clean Washer or hottest cycle, drum empty, with washer cleaner or bleach per your manual
5. Towel-dry the seal, door glass and drum
6. Leave the door and drawer open after every load, and unload wet laundry right away

⚠️ Wear gloves, ventilate, never use undiluted bleach on the seal, and NEVER mix bleach with vinegar or ammonia.
