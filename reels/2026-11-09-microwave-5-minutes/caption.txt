Stop scrubbing your microwave. Let lemon steam do the work 🍋

1. Add 1 cup of water to a microwave-safe bowl, squeeze in half a lemon and drop it in.
2. Heat on high for 3–5 minutes, until it boils and the window fogs.
3. Keep the door closed 5 more minutes so the steam loosens the splatter.
4. Oven mitts on, lift out the bowl, then wipe the top, walls and door with a soft sponge dipped in the lemon water.
5. Wash the turntable in warm soapy water, dry it and put it back.

Safety: the bowl and steam are very hot, so use mitts and open the door slowly. No metal, no steel wool, and never run the microwave empty.
