Hazy inside windshield? It's an oily film, not dust. Try the 2-towel trick for zero streaks 🚗

1. Park in the shade and wipe the glass with a dry microfiber towel to lift grit.
2. Spray ammonia-free auto glass cleaner on the towel, not on the glass. Lay a spare towel on the dash.
3. Clean one half at a time: side to side, then top to bottom.
4. Buff right away with a second clean, dry towel.
5. Check from a low angle and re-buff the bottom corners and top edge.

Safety: ammonia-free cleaner is tint-safe, keep spray away from the dash and screens, and never mix cleaners.
