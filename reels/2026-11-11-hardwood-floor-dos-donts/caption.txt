Stop soaking your wood floors. Do this instead 👇

1. Dry clean first: sweep, dust mop or vacuum on the hard-floor setting
2. Use a pH-neutral cleaner made for wood floors (test a hidden spot)
3. Mop damp, never wet: wring it well, small sections, with the grain
4. Dry as you go: no standing water, blot spills right away
5. Protect the finish: felt pads under furniture, mats at the doors

⚠️ Skip steam mops, vinegar, ammonia and bleach, and never mix cleaners. Waxed or unsealed floor? Follow your floor maker's care guide.
