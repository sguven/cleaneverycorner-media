Coffee on your cloth seat? Stop rubbing it. Do this instead 🚗

1. Vacuum the whole seat and the seams first.
2. Spot-test upholstery cleaner (or a few drops of dish soap in warm water) on a hidden area.
3. Lightly mist the stain. Don't soak it, or the foam underneath stays wet.
4. Work it in with a soft brush, using short strokes from the edges in.
5. Blot with a clean towel, don't rub. Repeat, then let it air-dry with the windows cracked.

Safety: keep the doors open for fresh air, no bleach on seat fabric, and never mix cleaners.
