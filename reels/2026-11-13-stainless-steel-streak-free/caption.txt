Streaky stainless fridge? Stop wiping in circles. Do this instead ✨

1. Find the grain: the faint lines show which way to wipe.
2. Dip a microfiber cloth in warm water with a few drops of dish soap and wring it out well.
3. Wipe with the grain in long strokes, never in circles.
4. Wipe again with a clean cloth dampened in plain water to lift the soap film.
5. Dry and buff with a dry microfiber, with the grain, so no water spots are left.

Safety: check your manual if it has a fingerprint-resistant coating, and skip bleach, vinegar, ammonia, steel wool, scouring pads and paper towels.
