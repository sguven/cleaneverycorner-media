When did you last clean your mattress? 😬 5 steps, no soaking:

1. Strip the bed and wash all bedding per the care label.
2. Vacuum the top, sides and seams with the upholstery tool.
3. Blot stains with dish soap suds on a cloth, then a damp cloth. Don't rub.
4. Sprinkle a thin layer of baking soda and let it sit a few hours (overnight is best, window open).
5. Vacuum it all up, let it air-dry fully, then add a mattress protector.

⚠️ Check the care tag first, never pour or spray liquid straight onto the mattress (especially memory foam), and never mix cleaners or use bleach.
