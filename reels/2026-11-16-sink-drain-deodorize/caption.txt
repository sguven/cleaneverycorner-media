Sink smells? It's probably not your drain. Check the splash guard 👀

1. Power off the disposal, then scrub both sides of the rubber splash guard with dish soap and a small brush.
2. Pour in 1/4 cup baking soda, wait 15 min, flush with hot tap water (not boiling).
3. Power back on and grind ice cubes + citrus peels with cold water running.

⚠️ Never put your hand inside the disposal, and skip bleach or chemical drain cleaners.
