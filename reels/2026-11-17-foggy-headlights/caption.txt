Toothpaste on foggy headlights? It looks great… for a few weeks. Here's the fix that lasts 🚗

1. Tape off the paint around the lens, then wet sand with fine grits, keeping it wet.
2. Polish with a plastic polish and a clean microfiber until it's clear.
3. Seal with a UV coat. This is the step that stops the yellow from coming back. Let it cure.

⚠️ Wear gloves and work with fresh air when you apply the sealant. Keep it off paint and trim, and follow your kit's directions.
