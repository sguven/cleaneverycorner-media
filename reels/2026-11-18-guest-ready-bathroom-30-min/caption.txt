Guests in 30 minutes and the bathroom looks like this? 😬 Here's the fast reset:

1. Clear the counter into a basket. Spray the toilet bowl and sink, then let the cleaner sit 2–3 minutes.
2. Wipe top to bottom: mirror, sink, then toilet last. Spray the cloth, not the mirror.
3. Mop the floor, hang a fresh hand towel and leave a spare roll where guests can see it.

⚠️ Run the fan or crack a window, wear gloves, and never mix bleach with ammonia, vinegar or toilet bowl cleaner.
