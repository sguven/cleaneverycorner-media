Is winter wrecking your leather seats? Those white rings are road salt — and the grit scratches every time you sit down 🚗

1️⃣ Vacuum first, especially the seams
2️⃣ Leather cleaner on a soft brush or microfiber (not sprayed on the seat), then wipe dry
3️⃣ Thin coat of leather conditioner, buff off the extra — about every 3 months

⚠️ No bleach, ammonia, glass cleaner or alcohol on leather. Test a hidden spot first, and let it dry away from direct heat.
