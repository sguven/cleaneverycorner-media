That sticky film on your cabinets? It's not dirt. It's cooking grease — and plain dish soap melts it 👀

1. A few drops of dish soap in warm water. Sponge damp, not dripping. Wipe with the grain.
2. Stubborn spot by the handle? Let the soapy water sit 1 minute, then use a soft toothbrush. Soft side of the sponge only.
3. Rinse with a clean damp cloth and dry right away with microfiber — leftover water can dull the finish.

Safety: test a hidden spot first and never mix cleaners.
