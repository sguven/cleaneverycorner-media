Stop putting tire shine on your floor mats. Shiny rubber = slippery pedals 😬 Here's the winter salt fix:

1. Pull the mats out, shake them, and hose off the slush and salt.
2. Mild soap + water and a stiff (not wire) brush in the grooves. Rinse well.
3. Dry them completely, then clip them back in place. Satin black is the goal, not glossy.

Safety: no tire shine, silicone dressing or floor wax on mats, and no bleach or solvents.
