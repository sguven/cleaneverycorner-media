Hosting Thanksgiving? Skip the dish mountain after dinner 🦃 3 rules:

1️⃣ Empty the dishwasher before you start, then load as you go.
2️⃣ Fill one side of the sink with hot soapy water. Wash knives and boards as you go.
3️⃣ Keep a trash bowl on the counter and wipe spills before they dry.

Food safety: don't rinse the raw turkey. After raw turkey, wash counters, boards and utensils with hot soapy water, then sanitize (USDA: 1 Tbsp unscented bleach per gallon of water, never mixed with other cleaners).
