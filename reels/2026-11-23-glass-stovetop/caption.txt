Don't scrub burnt-on gunk off a glass stovetop. Scrape it. 🔥
1️⃣ Let the cooktop cool, then dab a paste of baking soda + a little water on the burnt spots.
2️⃣ Hold a glass-safe razor scraper at about 45° and push the crust off with light pressure.
3️⃣ Wipe away the residue and buff dry with a soft microfiber cloth.
⚠️ Skip steel wool, abrasive powders, bleach and ammonia on glass tops (check your cooktop manual). Never mix cleaners.
