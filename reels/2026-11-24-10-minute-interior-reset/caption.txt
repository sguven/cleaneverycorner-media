You don't need a full detail before a road trip. You need 10 minutes. 🚗
1️⃣ Trash out, mats out: one bag, then shake the mats outside.
2️⃣ Vacuum top to bottom, before any spray: seat seams first, floor last.
3️⃣ Wipe what you touch (wheel, handles, cup holders) with cleaner sprayed on the towel. Glass goes last.
⚠️ Spray the towel, not the screen. Skip bleach and ammonia cleaners inside, and never mix products.
