The wine isn't what ruins your tablecloth. The dryer is. 🍷

1️⃣ Blot, don't rub. Flush with cold water from the back.
2️⃣ Gravy? Work in a drop of dish soap gently, rinse cold.
3️⃣ Soak in oxygen bleach (washable fabric, follow the label), wash, then air-dry and check. Heat sets what's left, so skip the dryer until it's gone.

⚠️ Check the care label, wear gloves, and never mix bleach with vinegar or ammonia.
