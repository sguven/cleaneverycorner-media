It's not your vacuum. The crumbs are wedged deep in the seat seams, where suction alone can't reach them 🚗
1️⃣ Slide the seat all the way back, then all the way forward, to expose the gaps and rails.
2️⃣ Spread the seam with your fingers and brush crumbs up and out with a soft detail brush.
3️⃣ Then vacuum slowly with the crevice tool: seam first, then the gap by the console.
⚠️ Skip knives and screwdrivers in the seams (they snag fabric). Using compressed air? Short bursts and eye protection.
